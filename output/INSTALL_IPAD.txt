INSTALLING THE SW HELI CONTENT PACK IN FOREFLIGHT (iPad)
=======================================================

One file holds everything: SW_Heli_ContentPack.zip (about 65 MB)
  - 3 landing overlays: SW Landable BLM, SW No-Land, SW Call-First
  - 7 POI map layers: waterfalls, arches, hoodoos & pillars, hot springs,
    craters & volcanic, glaciers, viewpoints
  - every POI as a ForeFlight user waypoint (IDs like WF0042, HS0007)

1. DOWNLOAD ON THE iPAD (Wi-Fi recommended)
   Open Safari and go to:
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gordonjs/echoesvoyage/claude/foreflight-landing-mbtiles-abhtph/output/SW_Heli_ContentPack.zip
   Tap "Download" when Safari asks. It lands in Files > Downloads.

2. HAND THE ZIP TO FOREFLIGHT (do NOT tap the zip in Files - that unzips it)
   Open the Files app > Downloads. Touch and hold SW_Heli_ContentPack.zip >
   Share > ForeFlight. If ForeFlight isn't in the row of app icons, swipe to
   the end and tap "More" to find it.
   (From a Mac instead: AirDrop the zip to the iPad and choose ForeFlight.)

3. CHECK THE IMPORT
   ForeFlight opens More > Custom Content. You should see the pack
   "SW Heli Landing & POI"; tap it to see the 10 map layers and the user
   waypoints.

4. TURN THE LAYERS ON
   Maps > tap the map-layer menu (top left). Choose a base map (e.g. VFR
   Sectional), then scroll to the bottom: the custom layers are listed there.
   Turn on what you want, for example:
     - SW No-Land + SW Landable BLM for landing planning
     - SW Call-First when state or USFS land is in play
     - any of the SW POI layers
   The overlays are semi-transparent and show from zoom 6 to 13; zooming in
   further just magnifies the z13 tiles.

5. USE THE POIs
   Tap a POI icon. The popup shows:
     - LAND HERE: LANDABLE / CALL FIRST / NO-LAND / NO-GO / OTHER
     - nearest landable BLM: distance, true bearing, ground elevation, lat/lon
     - ground elevation at the POI (USGS 3DEP)
     - flags: NPS no-landing, FAA AC 91-36D 2,000 ft AGL request,
       Grand Canyon SFRA, DoD land
     - the user-waypoint ID
   Icon centre colour = category; icon ring colour = landing class at that
   spot (same colours as the overlays; grey = NPS / DoD / private / other).
   To fly there, type the waypoint ID (e.g. WF0042) into Direct-To or the
   route editor. SW_POI.csv (in output/) lists every ID with its details.

6. QUICK SANITY CHECK
   Pan to the San Rafael Swell / Green River, UT: Labyrinth Canyon and Muddy
   Creek wilderness should be red, the Dirty Devil WSA (south-east of Hanksville)
   orange, and the BLM land around them green.

7. UPDATING LATER
   To replace the pack with a newer build: More > Custom Content, delete
   "SW Heli Landing & POI", then import the new zip the same way.

Everything is stored on the iPad and works offline in flight.
